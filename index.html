Projects

Live product, 2026

Bible Typing Indonesia

Keyboard-first typing practice for the Indonesian AYT Bible. All 66 books and 31,102 verses ship inside the app, verified byte-for-byte against the published source, so every passage you type is the real text.

  • Three modes: pick any verse, walk through a book, or resume where you stopped, with live gross and net WPM and accuracy
  • Saved verses with search, per-passage progress, and a settings panel from caret style to key sounds generated with the Web Audio API
  • No account and no server: progress lives entirely in your browser across a 31,102-verse dataset
